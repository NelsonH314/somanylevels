I actually teared up finding this one. The memories came flooding back to me quite literally. Age of War 2 was another tower defense game where you battled against a computer opponent through different ages of time. You start back in the caveman age and work your way up to the future as you gained Xp. My siblings and I would play this game for hours on end. Altough, I was never able to do the immpossible mode. I guess that is why they call it impossible.

As a very passionate fan of this game however, I do have to point out how much I disagree with this player's strategy. He started off great buiilding the chicket turret first but once he gets the the final stage, HE DELETES ALL OF HIS TURRETS! Turrets were always my main strategy. In fact sometimes I wouldn't send more soldiers to the enemies place for a couple of evolutions just to save up gold to speed run things once you get to the final stage. That being said, this game was a little more niche, so I will take what gameplay I can get.
