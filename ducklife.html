Training ducks to race. I mean, it doesn't get any better than that. In this game, you give your duck a name, feed them some food, and train them in various minigames. You could train them in running, swimming, flying, and climbing. Once trained, they can enter races to be the greatest duck of them all. This franchise has 9 games as of 2026.

Duck Life 3 was the game with the mildly unsettling evolved ducks. You could have your duck specialize in one of the 4 training areas and they would evolve into ducks with attributes to better assist in that area. This lead to ducks with long legs and even fish fins.
